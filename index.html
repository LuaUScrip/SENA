03 / Key

Keys

The script works without a key. A key sets your rank and unlocks anything marked for donators.

  1. Join the Discord

    Keys are handed out there by staff.

  2. Run the loader

    Copy it from the top of this page and execute it inside the game.

  3. Redeem in game

    Open the Donate tab in the NOX window, paste your key and press Redeem.
